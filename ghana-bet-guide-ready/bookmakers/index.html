What should you compare before choosing a betting site?

Use the same checks for every operator instead of relying on advertising claims or a headline bonus.

1. Availability and licensing

First, check whether the operator accepts customers in Ghana and whether its relevant business and service are authorised under applicable local requirements. Do not treat a working website, a brand name or an advertising campaign as proof of licensing.

Look for the legal company name, relevant licence information and current terms. Where possible, cross-check the information against the official regulator's records. If a detail cannot be verified, treat it as unconfirmed.

2. Mobile Money and other payment methods

Payment options may vary by operator, account and location. Check the official cashier for the methods available to you, including whether services such as MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash or other payment options are actually supported.

Check minimum and maximum amounts, any fees, payment processing rules and the conditions that apply to your account.

Read our Mobile Money payment guide →

3. Deposits and withdrawals

A method available for deposits may not necessarily be available for withdrawals. Review withdrawal methods, identity verification requirements, limits, fees and the operator's process for delayed or rejected transactions before depositing money.

4. Welcome offers and promotional terms

A headline bonus does not tell you the full conditions. Read the official promotion rules and check eligibility, minimum deposit, wagering requirements, expiry dates, maximum qualifying amounts and any restrictions on withdrawals.

We do not publish a specific bonus amount as current unless it can be checked against a relevant, current source. Promotional terms can change without notice.

5. Mobile apps and website usability

Check whether the operator offers an official app for your device and location. Download applications only from trusted official sources. Review the permissions requested and avoid unofficial downloads or links sent by unknown people.

Read our betting apps guide →

6. Customer support and account security

Before registering, find the operator's official support channels and read its account security guidance. Never share your password, Mobile Money PIN or one-time verification code with another person.
