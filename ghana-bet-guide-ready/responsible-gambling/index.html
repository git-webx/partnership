Responsible gambling

Gambling can be addictive and can cause financial and personal harm. Betting is not a reliable way to earn money.

Keep it in perspective

  • Only participate if you are 18 or older and it is legal for you to do so.
  • Never use money needed for food, housing, bills, debt payments or family essentials.
  • Set time and spending limits before you start, and do not chase losses.
  • Do not borrow money to gamble or treat betting as a source of income.
  • Take a break if gambling affects your mood, relationships, work or finances.
  • Use account limits, time-outs or self-exclusion tools where available.

If gambling stops feeling manageable

Speak with someone you trust and seek support from a qualified local health or support service. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This site does not currently list a verified Ghana-specific support hotline, so we do not publish an unverified number.
