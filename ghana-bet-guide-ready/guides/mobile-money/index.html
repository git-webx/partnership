Mobile Money payment guide

Mobile Money services and operator payment options can vary. This guide explains what to check; it does not confirm that any particular operator supports a payment method.

Check these details first

  • Is the payment method shown inside the operator's official cashier for your account and location?
  • What are the minimum and maximum transaction amounts?
  • Are there provider or operator fees?
  • What is the expected processing time, and how are failed transactions handled?
  • Must the payment account be registered in your own name?
  • What records or transaction references should you keep?

Protect your account

Never give another person your Mobile Money PIN or one-time passcode. Use only official payment flows and avoid agents who ask you to send funds to a personal number. If money is debited but not credited, save the transaction reference and contact the payment provider and operator through their official channels.

Important: No fees, limits or payout timelines are stated here because they must be checked against current official terms.
