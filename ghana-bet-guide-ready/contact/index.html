Business & Advertising Enquiries

Ghana Bet Guide is an independent information website covering betting operators and practical betting-related topics for adults in Ghana.

We welcome relevant proposals from betting brands, affiliate programmes, marketing agencies and other businesses interested in reaching an audience interested in betting information in Ghana.

When contacting us about a commercial opportunity, please include:

  • Your name and company or brand.
  • Your company website and the market you serve.
  • The type of partnership or advertising you are proposing.
  • Relevant affiliate programme details or campaign information.
  • Your proposed commercial terms, if available.

All proposals are subject to review. Contacting us does not guarantee publication, a listing, a particular placement or a partnership agreement.

Email: ghana.bet.guide@info.com

For advertising or affiliate proposals, please use a clear subject line so we can identify the purpose of your enquiry.

Editorial Independence

Commercial proposals are considered separately from the accuracy of our informational content. Payment or an affiliate relationship does not guarantee a positive review or a particular editorial position.

Some links on Ghana Bet Guide may generate affiliate commissions. For more information, see our Affiliate Disclosure.

Betting Account & Payment Support

Ghana Bet Guide is an independent information resource, not a betting operator or payment provider. We cannot access betting accounts, process deposits or withdrawals, or resolve account disputes on behalf of operators.

For account access, deposits, withdrawals or verification problems, contact the relevant operator through its official support channels. For regulatory information, consult the Gaming Commission of Ghana.
